# Notes

Working notes kept by Claude (Anthropic's AI model, in Claude Code) while
doing this work with Behdad Esfahbod in November 2026. In them "I" is Claude
and "he" is Behdad.

- `decisions.md`: what was decided about the note and the page, and why.
- `todo.md`: what is left, and things found on the way that were not acted on.
- `kinks.md`: the two measurements of kinks at smooth joints of interpolated
  masters.
- `research-agents.md`: the reports of three research runs (other converters
  and prior art; a better error measure; better weights).
- `closed-form.md`, `fonttools.md`, `kurbo.md`, `harfbuzz-one-quadratic.md`:
  notes from the work on HarfBuzz, fontTools and kurbo that the note grew out
  of, as they were written at the time.

They are notes, not findings. What the note itself states was checked again
by the scripts in `scripts/`; much of the rest here was measured once, by an
AI agent, and not reviewed. Numbers about other software in particular come
from reimplementations made for the comparison, not from that software, and
should not be quoted as results about it.
